About The Association

Organized monthly contribution management for all members

Every active member contributes 200 BDT each month. To keep the responsibility balanced, two active members are assigned each month to collect contributions from everyone.

Association Rules

  • Contribution amount: 200 BDT
  • Two collectors are assigned every month
  • New members need admin approval
  • Admin can manage members and payment status
